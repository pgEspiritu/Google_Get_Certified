## 2. 🎭 Role Prompting

**Question:**  
You are designing a language learning app and want to incorporate an AI tutor that can provide personalized feedback to students practicing their conversational skills. Which role-prompt would be most effective in guiding the AI's interactions with students?

**Options:**
- **"You are a helpful and encouraging tutor who provides constructive feedback on language learning exercises."** ✅
- "You are a language model built to help students learn a new language."
- "You are a robot programmed to assess language proficiency."
- "Provide personalized feedback to students practicing conversational skills."

**Correct Answer:** "You are a helpful and encouraging tutor who provides constructive feedback on language learning exercises."

**Explanation:**
- An effective role prompt explicitly defines the AI's persona and expected behavior.
- A good role prompt includes:
  - **Persona:** Helpful and encouraging tutor.
  - **Task:** Provide constructive feedback.
  - **Context:** Language learning exercises.
- This guides the AI's communication style and the type of assistance it provides.

- It enables complex tasks like planning a business trip itinerary.
- It improves the AI model's ability to understand complex questions.

**Explanation:**

  - **Focus:** The perspective or expertise applied to the task.
- Example:

## 4. 🚀 Zero-Shot Prompting

**Question:**  
A developer wants an AI model to perform a task using only its pre-existing knowledge, without providing any example input-output pairs in the prompt. Which prompting technique describes this approach?

**Options:**
- **Zero-shot prompting** ✅
- Few-shot prompting
- One-shot prompting
- Multi-shot prompting

**Correct Answer:** Zero-shot prompting

**Explanation:**
- Zero-shot prompting asks the AI to perform a task without providing examples.
- The model relies on its existing knowledge and ability to understand instructions.

**Prompting Techniques Comparison:**

| Technique | Description |
|---|---|
| Zero-shot | No examples are provided. |
| One-shot | One example is provided. |
| Few-shot | A small number of examples are provided. |
| Multi-shot | Multiple examples are provided. |
