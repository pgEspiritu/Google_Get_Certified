## 3. 🔎 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

**Question:**  
What is the primary function of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) in AI models?

**Options:**
- To replace human intervention in the AI model's output generation process.
- **To enable the AI model to access and utilize external knowledge sources for generating outputs.** ✅
- To enhance the AI model's ability to memorize vast amounts of information.
- To restrict the AI model's output to a predefined set of responses.

**Correct Answer:** To enable the AI model to access and utilize external knowledge sources for generating outputs.

- RAG combines information retrieval with AI-generated responses.
- It works through two main stages:
  1. **Retrieval:** Finds relevant information from external knowledge sources.
  2. **Generation:** Uses the retrieved information as context to generate a response.
- RAG helps provide more relevant and context-aware answers without relying solely on the model's existing knowledge.
