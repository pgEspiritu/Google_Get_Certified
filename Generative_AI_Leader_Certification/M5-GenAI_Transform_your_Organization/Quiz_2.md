
# 📝 Quiz 2 – Generative AI Agents

**Passing Score:** 80%

## 1. Which core capability of Agent Search helps mitigate hallucinations in generative AI, ensuring that search results are grounded in reliable information?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer: A</badge> **Grounding**

- Grounding connects AI-generated responses to reliable, relevant information sources.
- It helps reduce hallucinations by basing responses on retrieved evidence rather than relying solely on the model's internal knowledge.

## 2. What is a key advantage of using a hybrid approach (combining deterministic and generative AI) when building conversational agents with Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer: A</badge> **It enables strict control over agent behavior while leveraging the flexibility of generative AI.**

- Deterministic AI provides predefined rules and predictable actions for sensitive or regulated processes.
- Generative AI provides flexibility, natural conversation, and reasoning.
- Combining them balances control, compliance, and conversational flexibility.

## 3. According to the provided information, how does an LLM interact with data stores in a RAG workflow?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer: B</badge> **The LLM queries data stores to retrieve information relevant to a user's request, enriching its response.**

- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) allows an LLM to retrieve relevant external information.
- Retrieved information is added to the prompt as context before generating a response.
- **Important:** RAG does not permanently modify or expand the model's training knowledge or parameters.

## 4. What are the key steps in planning for impact when implementing generative AI solutions?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer: C</badge> **Define key metrics, collect and analyze data, and iterate and improve.**

- **Define key metrics:** Establish measurable indicators of success.
- **Collect and analyze data:** Evaluate actual performance and outcomes.
- **Iterate and improve:** Use findings to refine the solution and maximize its impact.

## 5. What is the primary purpose of Gemini Enterprise app within an organization?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer: C</badge> **To provide a centralized platform for organizing and accessing AI agents that can utilize company data to assist employees.**

- Gemini Enterprise app serves as a central hub for enterprise-ready AI agents.
- It connects AI capabilities with company information across different business systems.
- It helps employees discover information, conduct research, analyze data, and automate tasks.

## 6. Which option best identifies the key components of an AI agent?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer: D</badge> **A reasoning loop, tools, and a foundational model.**

- **Foundational model:** The AI agent's intelligence or brain.
- **Tools:** Enable the agent to access information and perform actions.
- **Reasoning loop:** Guides the agent's thinking, decisions, and actions toward achieving its goal.
