
# 🔁 Adding Prompt Engineering to Your Reasoning Loops

## 📖 Introduction

Prompt engineering helps guide how generative AI agents reason, plan, and take actions to achieve their goals.

## 🧠 The Reasoning Loop

The **reasoning loop** is a key component of a generative AI agent. It governs how the agent:

1. Receives and processes information.
2. Performs internal reasoning.
3. Uses its reasoning to determine the next action or decision.
4. Evaluates the result and continues working toward its goal.

- It is an **iterative and introspective process**, not a one-time operation.
- It continues until the agent achieves its goal or reaches a stopping point.
- Its complexity depends on the agent and the task being performed.

### 🔑 Key Aspects of the Reasoning Loop

#### 1. 🔄 Iterative Process

- The agent continuously evaluates its progress.
- It determines the next best action based on the information available.
- The cycle involves:
  - **Action** – Performing an operation.
  - **Tool Selection** – Choosing the appropriate tool.
  - **Observation** – Examining the result of the action.

#### 2. 🧩 Internal Reasoning

- The agent uses its underlying language model to reason through the steps needed to complete a task.
- The language model provides reasoning and logical capabilities.
- It helps the agent determine how to approach a problem.

#### 3. 🎯 Decision Making

- The agent uses its internal reasoning to decide what to do next.
- This includes:
  - Selecting the appropriate tools.
  - Determining the required inputs for those tools.
  - Choosing the next course of action based on available information.

#### 4. 📝 Reasoning Frameworks

- The reasoning loop uses **prompt engineering frameworks and techniques** to guide the agent's reasoning and planning.
- These frameworks help structure how the agent:
  - Approaches a task.
  - Plans its actions.
  - Selects tools.
  - Evaluates results.

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- The reasoning loop enables an AI agent to work iteratively toward a goal.
- The underlying language model provides reasoning and logic capabilities.
- The agent makes decisions based on its reasoning and observations.
- Tool selection and action execution are important parts of the loop.
- Prompt engineering frameworks help guide the agent's reasoning, planning, and decision-making.
