## 1. Who is responsible for building and deploying custom AI agents and integrating AI capabilities into applications?

**Correct Answer: Developers** ✅

**Explanation:**

Developers build and deploy custom AI agents, integrate AI capabilities into existing applications, and connect AI services through APIs.

- **Network engineers:** Focus on network infrastructure, connectivity, and performance.
- **AI practitioners:** Specialize in training, customizing, evaluating, and optimizing AI models.
- **Business leaders:** Identify business needs and use AI solutions to improve operations.
- **Developers:** Build applications, integrate AI capabilities, and deploy custom agents.

💡 **Remember:** Developers build AI-powered applications, while AI practitioners focus more on the underlying models.

## 2. What is Gemini Nano designed for?

**Correct Answer: Deploying AI on edge devices and smartphones** ✅

**Explanation:**

Gemini Nano is Google's compact and efficient AI model designed to run directly on devices such as smartphones and other supported edge devices.

Benefits include:

- 🔒 **Privacy:** Data can be processed locally on the device.
- ⚡ **Speed:** Reduces the need to send requests to remote cloud servers.
- 📵 **Offline Access:** Supports certain AI capabilities without continuous internet connectivity, depending on the device and feature.

💡 **Remember:** Gemini Nano brings AI capabilities directly to supported devices instead of relying entirely on cloud-based processing.

## 3. What is the primary advantage of edge computing for AI applications?

**Correct Answer: Real-time responsiveness and reduced latency** ✅

**Explanation:**

Edge computing processes data on devices or servers closer to the source of the data or point of need.

This reduces the time required to send data to a remote cloud server and receive a response.

**Example:** A self-driving vehicle needs to recognize obstacles and react immediately. Processing AI tasks locally helps it make decisions without waiting for cloud communication.

Other benefits include:
- Reduced dependence on internet connectivity.
- Improved privacy through local processing.
- Faster responses for time-sensitive applications.

💡 **Remember:** Edge computing brings processing closer to the data source, reducing latency and improving responsiveness.

## 4. Your marketing team needs to quickly generate engaging product descriptions for an upcoming ecommerce sale. Which approach is the most efficient way to achieve this?

**Correct Answer: Use a Gen AI-powered application like Google Workspace with Gemini to draft the descriptions.** ✅

**Explanation:**

The marketing team needs to generate content quickly. Using an existing Gen AI application is the most efficient approach because it avoids the time, cost, and technical effort involved in developing or customizing a model.

Why the other options are less efficient:

- **Hire copywriters:** Requires manual effort and may take more time to produce many descriptions.
- **Fine-tune a pre-trained model:** Requires additional data preparation, training, evaluation, and resources.
- **Build a custom model from scratch:** Requires substantial technical expertise, data, computing resources, and development time.
- **Use Google Workspace with Gemini:** Provides an existing AI-powered tool that can quickly generate and refine product descriptions.

💡 **Remember:** For straightforward content-generation tasks, start with an existing Gen AI application rather than building a custom model.
