## 2. Which of the following defines generative AI?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer</badge>

- ✅ **A type of artificial intelligence that can create new content, including images, text, and music.**

**Key Learning:**
- Generative AI creates new content based on patterns learned from training data.
- It can generate different types of content, including:
  - ✍️ Text
  - 🖼️ Images
  - 🎵 Music and audio
  - 🎬 Video
  - 💻 Code

**Remember:** Generative AI goes beyond analyzing existing data. It can also produce new content.

<badge color="success">Correct Answer</badge>

- ✅ **Creating photorealistic images of new product prototypes based on text descriptions.**

**Key Learning:**
- Generative AI has applications beyond chatbots and customer support.
- Businesses can use it for:
  - 🎨 Product design and image generation.
  - 📢 Marketing and advertising content.
  - 📝 Report and document creation.
  - 💡 Brainstorming and creative development.
  - 💻 Code generation and software development.

**Remember:** Generative AI can support creativity, innovation, and business operations—not just conversations.

## 4. Which example describes a multimodal generative AI application?

<badge color="success">Correct Answer</badge>

- ✅ **Using generative AI to analyze customer sentiment in video testimonials and survey data.**

**Key Learning:**
- **Multimodal AI** can process and work with multiple types of data or information.
- In this example, the application combines:
  - 🎥 Video testimonials – visual and audio information.
  - 📄 Survey responses – text information.
- By analyzing these different inputs together, the AI can help identify customer sentiment.

**Remember:** A multimodal application combines multiple modalities, such as text, images, audio, and video, depending on the model's capabilities.
