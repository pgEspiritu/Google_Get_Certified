# 💡 Brainstorming Generative AI Use Cases

## 🧠 Introduction: Identifying Gen AI Opportunities

- Brainstorming and prioritizing practical generative AI use cases can be challenging for organizations.
- One recommended method is the **Creative Matrix**, a structured brainstorming technique that helps teams systematically generate ideas by combining different aspects of AI capabilities with business needs.
- The goal is to identify how generative AI can address actual organizational challenges and support business priorities.

## 🧩 1. What Is a Creative Matrix?

- A **Creative Matrix** is a brainstorming framework that uses two axes to generate ideas by exploring the intersection of different categories.
- It encourages teams to think creatively about how AI capabilities can be applied to specific business activities.

### How to Build a Creative Matrix

1. **Assemble a Team**
   - Bring together employees, managers, and other stakeholders who understand the organization's operations and challenges.

2. **Create Two Axes**
   - Identify two dimensions that will guide the brainstorming activity.

3. **Define the First Axis: Gen AI Solutions**
   - List the available AI capabilities or solutions.
   - These can be organized by:
     - Gen AI products or tools.
     - AI modalities (text, image, audio, video).
     - AI capabilities or technologies.

4. **Define the Second Axis: Business-Specific Areas**
   - Identify the organization's needs and priorities.
   - These can include:
     - Business personas or customer groups.
     - Existing workflows.
     - Business processes.
     - Strategic goals.
     - Operational challenges.

5. **Generate Ideas Using Sticky Notes**
   - Ask team members to think of possible applications where the two axes intersect.
   - Write each idea on a sticky note, either physically or using a virtual collaboration tool.
   - Place each idea in the appropriate cell of the matrix.

6. **Evaluate and Prioritize Ideas**
   - Review each proposed solution.
   - Assess its feasibility, expected impact, and development effort.
   - Prioritize ideas that are practical and provide significant value.

> 💡 **Key Insight:** A Creative Matrix helps organizations move from general ideas about AI to specific use cases that address real business needs.

## 📊 2. Creative Matrix Example: Faux Floral Business

- Consider a fictional business that sells artificial or faux floral arrangements.
- The team wants to explore how Google Cloud's generative AI capabilities can support its business priorities.

### Example Matrix Dimensions

- **Business Priorities:**
  - Drive innovation.
  - Enhance customer experience.
  - Increase operational efficiency.

- **Gen AI Capabilities:**
  - Agent Search.
  - Agent Conversation.
  - Agent Studio.

### Examples of AI Use Cases

**🔎 A. Drive Innovation + Agent Search**

- Develop an AI-powered product search tool.
- Allow customers to upload images of floral arrangements they like.
- Use image-based search to identify similar faux floral products in the business catalog.
- Help customers discover products more easily through visual search.

**Example Application:**
- A customer uploads a picture of a floral arrangement.
- The AI-powered search tool identifies visually similar products available in the store.

**💬 B. Enhance Customer Experience + Agent Conversation**

- Develop an AI-powered chatbot to provide instant customer support.
- The chatbot can answer questions about:
  - Faux floral care.
  - Product arrangements.
  - Product availability.
  - Ordering procedures.
- Provide customers with assistance without requiring an employee to answer every inquiry manually.

**Example Application:**
- A customer asks, "How do I clean and maintain my artificial flowers?"
- The AI assistant provides relevant care instructions based on the business's product information.

**📈 C. Increase Efficiency + Agent Studio**

- Use AI capabilities to analyze customer data and predict future demand for different faux floral products.
- Support business planning and inventory management.
- Help the business anticipate which products may become more popular.

**Example Application:**
- The business analyzes historical sales and customer demand.
- An AI solution identifies demand patterns and helps forecast which floral arrangements may need additional inventory.

## 🎯 3. Evaluating and Prioritizing Gen AI Use Cases

- After completing the Creative Matrix, review each sticky note and assess whether the proposed solution is worth pursuing.
- Two important evaluation dimensions are:

### 📊 Impact

- Measures the expected value or benefits of implementing the AI solution.
- Consider:
  - Improved customer experience.
  - Increased productivity.
  - Reduced operational costs.
  - Increased revenue.
  - Faster service delivery.
  - Better decision-making.

### 🛠️ Feasibility and Development Effort

- Measures how practical and achievable the proposed solution is.
- Consider:
  - Availability and quality of data.
  - Required technology and infrastructure.
  - Development complexity.
  - Financial resources.
  - Employee skills and expertise.
  - Security, privacy, and regulatory requirements.
  - Time needed for implementation.

### Example Prioritization Matrix

| Priority | Expected Impact | Development Effort | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 High Priority | High | Low | Implement or pilot first. |
| 🟡 Evaluate Further | High | High | Assess resources, risks, and long-term value. |
| 🟡 Quick Improvement | Low | Low | Consider if resources are available. |
| 🔴 Low Priority | Low | High | Deprioritize or reconsider the use case. |

> 💡 **Key Insight:** Prioritize solutions that offer high business impact and are reasonably feasible to implement. A technically impressive AI solution is not necessarily the best investment if it does not address an important business need.

## 🤖 4. Using Gemini to Support Brainstorming

- The **Gemini app** can help teams generate additional ideas and explore possible Gen AI applications.
- It can assist with:
  - Identifying potential AI use cases.
  - Suggesting solutions for operational challenges.
  - Exploring different AI capabilities.
  - Expanding initial brainstorming ideas.
  - Evaluating potential benefits and limitations.

### Example Prompt

"Act as a generative AI strategy consultant. Our organization wants to improve operational efficiency, customer service, and data-driven decision-making. Suggest five practical generative AI use cases. For each use case, identify the business problem, proposed AI solution, expected benefits, required data, implementation challenges, and estimated feasibility."

**Important Reminder:**
- AI-generated ideas should be validated before implementation.
- Check whether the proposed solution addresses an actual business problem.
- Verify data availability, technical feasibility, costs, and potential risks.
- Consult relevant employees and stakeholders before proceeding.

## 📝 5. Practical Exercise: Generate Your Own Use Cases

- Review a sample Creative Matrix and develop **3–5 sticky-note ideas** of your own.
- Think about how different Gen AI capabilities can address your organization's actual needs.

### Suggested Brainstorming Process

1. Identify an organizational priority or operational problem.
2. Select a relevant Gen AI capability.
3. Think of a practical application that combines the two.
4. Describe the proposed solution in one sticky note.
5. Evaluate its expected impact and feasibility.
6. Validate the idea with relevant stakeholders.

### Sample Sticky-Note Ideas for a Government Organization

| Business Priority | Gen AI Capability | Proposed Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| 📄 Document Management | Text Generation and Summarization | Automatically summarize lengthy reports and extract key findings. |
| 📊 Data Analysis | Data Interpretation | Assist analysts in interpreting statistical tables and identifying trends. |
| 📧 Communication | Text Generation | Draft formal emails, memoranda, and other administrative communications. |
| 🎓 Employee Development | Conversational AI | Develop an AI assistant that answers questions about training materials and institutional guidelines. |
| 📚 Research and Knowledge Management | AI-Powered Search | Search institutional documents and retrieve relevant information using natural-language questions. |

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

- The **Creative Matrix** is a structured brainstorming method for generating practical Gen AI use cases.
- It combines AI capabilities with business priorities, workflows, personas, or operational challenges.
- Teams can use sticky notes to generate and organize ideas.
- Gemini can support brainstorming, but all generated ideas must be validated.
- Evaluate each proposed use case based on its expected impact, feasibility, and development effort.
- Prioritize solutions that address real business problems and align with organizational goals.
- Successful AI adoption begins with practical, valuable use cases rather than simply adopting technology for its own sake.
